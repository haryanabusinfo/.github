## 📌 About Haryana Bus Info

**Haryana Bus Info** is an independent digital transit research and schedule portal founded by **Manish Sharma**. Our mission is to modernize, verify, and make public transportation timetables universally accessible for over 4,000+ daily buses operated by **Haryana Roadways** and inter-state partner corporations.

We bridge the information gap for millions of regional commuters by cataloging:
- 🕒 **Depot Timetables & Schedules**: Direct, verified timings from 24+ Haryana Roadways depots.
- 🛣️ **Intercity & Interstate Corridors**: Routes connecting Hisar, Gurugram, Rohtak, Ambala, Chandigarh, Delhi (ISBT Kashmiri Gate, Sarai Kale Khan), Jaipur, and Himachal.
- 🎫 **Fare Estimates & Distance Matrices**: Accurate fare structures and route stops.
- 📞 **Depot Helpdesk Directories**: Centralized contact directories for official depot inquiries.

## 🛠️ Open Tech & Transit Standards

We are building lightweight open-source utilities to help transit researchers, developers, and daily commuters:

- **GTFS & Route Mapping**: Working towards standardized General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) feeds for regional Haryana bus corridors.
- **REST APIs & Microservices**: Scalable timetable lookup engines designed for low-latency delivery on mobile networks.
- **Structured Knowledge Graph**: Fully aligned with [Schema.org Transit](https://schema.org/BusTrip) and machine-readable Linked Data.
